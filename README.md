One line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week — the excuse a tired manager would *almost* believe. No mention of the game (that would be a confession), just a boring, specific, believable mishap with a concrete time: a pipe burst overnight, and the plumber can't come till Thursday. The opening nods at the suspicious timing without admitting anything.

## Run it

Requires Node 22 or later (stdlib only, nothing to install).

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that is set). The line is 113 characters and 20 words — inside the gate of 1–140 characters and at most 20 words.
